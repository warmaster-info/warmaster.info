A {dragon} can fly, increasing its
rider’s move to 100cm, and it adds [.edit]#+3/3# Attacks to those of its rider.
+
[.edit]#The {dragon} can breath fire if the character has
joined a unit that isn’t engaged in combat.#
This is a shooting
attack with a range of 20cm, which can be directed against one
target as normal. The breath has 3 Attacks, which are worked
out in xref:rules:characters.adoc#characters-that-shoot[the usual way].
+
Any unit joined by a character riding a {dragon} causes
terror in its enemies
