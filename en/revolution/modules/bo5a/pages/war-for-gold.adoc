= This War for Gold
:page-role: page=81

Before the Goblin army arrives at the Lonely Mountain, the
Elves, Men and Dwarves fight each other for possession of
Thorin’s gold. In this scenario, the Goblins are running a little
late so the Battle of Five Armies becomes the Battle of Three
Armies with Elves & Men on one side and Dwarves on the other.

This battle is fought over the same battlefield as the Battle of
Five Armies. To make more of a fight of it, double the number of
units on each side compared to the Battle of Five Armies (or
more if you want a really big game). We shall also add one unit
of Dwarf Archers to give the Dwarf player some missile fire. The
Elves and Men can set-up anywhere on the spurs, the Dwarves
deploy at the table edge marked ‘Evil Cavalry’ on the main map.
Thorin deploys behind his wall as usual. The Elfking is the Elf
side’s General and Dáin is the Dwarf General. Gandalf, the
Eagles and Beorn play no part in the game.
